# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project aims to provide a respectful, inclusive, professional, and
constructive environment for contributors, maintainers, reviewers, co-authors,
issue participants, pull-request participants, and users engaging through
repository channels.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Strong review, careful evidence, and direct
questions make the project better when they are expressed with respect for the
people doing the work.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and keep feedback focused on the project and the
  change being discussed.
- Give actionable, evidence-based feedback and explain blocking concerns
  clearly.
- Assume good intent where reasonable while still addressing harmful behaviour
  when it happens.
- Accept constructive review and respond to requested changes professionally.
- Acknowledge contributions accurately, including agreed authorship and
  co-author attribution.
- Protect private and sensitive information.
- Avoid publishing credentials, personal data, private correspondence, or
  security-sensitive details in issues, pull requests, logs, screenshots, or
  documentation.
- Help maintain a technically rigorous and welcoming project.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Personal attacks, insulting comments, or demeaning comments about a person
  rather than the work.
- Using technical review as a pretext for hostility.
- Deliberately misrepresenting another person's work.
- Knowingly false authorship or co-authorship claims.
- Dishonestly removing or fabricating contribution credit.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Exposing credentials, secrets, or sensitive security information.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer direction after a clear warning.

## Review and Collaboration Conduct

Pull-request discussion should help the current change become correct,
maintainable, and appropriately scoped. Reviewers may request clearer evidence,
validation output, narrower scope, or changes to implementation details.
Contributors should answer those requests in good faith and should not pressure
reviewers to approve incomplete work.

Feedback should stay focused on code, tests, documentation, security,
operability, and repository workflow rather than personal traits or motives.
Disagreements about implementation are expected; participants should make the
tradeoffs visible and let maintainers decide when a direction is settled.

Review and approval do not automatically create co-authorship. Co-author credit
should reflect genuine contribution to the committed work. Legitimate
contribution credit must not be removed dishonestly, and co-author credit must
not be fabricated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, reviews, discussions when enabled, repository documentation, and
project-related communication conducted through official or maintainer-approved
channels.

It does not claim authority over unrelated spaces or private communication that
is not connected to participation in this project.

## Reporting Concerns

For conduct concerns, contact a project maintainer through an available private
GitHub-supported or maintainer-approved channel. Do not include sensitive
personal information in a public issue.

This repository does not currently publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting
channel. If only public repository channels are available, keep the report
minimal, avoid private details, and ask a maintainer for a private follow-up
path.

Security-sensitive findings should be handled separately from interpersonal
conduct concerns. Do not publish vulnerabilities, credentials, exploit details,
or sensitive logs in public issues or pull requests. Use an available private
GitHub-supported or maintainer-approved channel for security matters when one is
available.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request edits or removal of inappropriate
content, close or lock disruptive discussions, reject contributions that violate
project standards, and restrict participation temporarily or permanently.

Maintainer responses should consider severity, context, impact, repetition, and
the participant's willingness to correct the behaviour. Serious conduct may
justify immediate stronger action.

## Enforcement Principles

Enforcement should be proportionate and focused on protecting the project
community and its work. Depending on the situation, maintainers may use:

1. Clarification or private guidance.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary participation restriction.
4. Permanent restriction for serious or repeated violations.

These steps are guidelines, not a required sequence. Severe or urgent behaviour
may require immediate restriction.
